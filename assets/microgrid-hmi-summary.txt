DC Microgrid HMI Prototype

A conceptual control-room display for normal operation, grid outage, and emergency disconnect scenarios.

Developed the proposal display and scenario-based interface. The prototype makes the chosen operating state visible through equipment labels, flows, and illustrative readings.

All values, device states, controls, and thresholds are simulated. No equipment is connected. This is an interface concept, not a validated engineering model, operational SCADA installation, or safety system.

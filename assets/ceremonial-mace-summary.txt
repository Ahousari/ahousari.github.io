Greenville Technical College Ceremonial Mace

A ceremonial mace designed through more than 100 hours of CAD and manufacturing iteration, with a finished brass/aluminum assembly.

Contributed more than 100 hours of CAD and design-for-manufacturability iteration with machinists, supporting fabrication and finishing of the multi-part brass/aluminum assembly.

Designed with Andrew Duvall and Adam McArdle, with machinists collaborating on fabrication and finishing.

Greenville Technical College Ceremonial Mace

A ceremonial mace designed through more than 100 hours of CAD and manufacturing iteration, with a finished brass/aluminum assembly.

Designed the mace through more than 100 hours of CAD and design-for-manufacturability iteration with machinists, supporting fabrication and finishing of the multi-part brass/aluminum assembly.

The images show the supplied CAD design and finished piece. Fabrication involved collaboration with machinists; design ownership does not imply that every component was personally machined.

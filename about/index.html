Abdurrahman Housari

I’m an electrical engineering honors student at Clemson University, graduating in December 2026. I enjoy work that connects physical systems, data, and software—from a circuit on a workbench to an application someone can use.

My experience spans industrial automation and robotics, thermal imaging and acquisition, CAD and manufacturing, and engineering lab instruction. My projects are a way to keep exploring: sometimes that means a processor in Logisim, sometimes an audio application, and sometimes a ceremonial mace.

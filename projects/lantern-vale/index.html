What I built

Lantern Vale brings together exploration, characters, equipment, and several encounter types in a browser-based adventure. The project includes an earlier demo, Chapter One, and a newer ten-chapter campaign, with its own progression and save handling.

My contribution

Developed and iterated the playable systems, browser interface, campaign structure, character/equipment behavior, and save workflows. The campaign combines platform routes, turn-based party battles, tower defense, and formation battles.

  • A ten-chapter campaign with multiple encounter modes.
  • Party recruitment, equipment choices, and progression.
  • Separate saves for earlier and newer play modes.
  • Keyboard and touch-oriented controls, with a shorter preview/quick-play path.

Components

Development history

Earlier demo, Chapter One, and newer campaign.

Game systems

Exploration, party encounters, equipment, progression, and browser saves.

Design choices

  • Keep campaign progress separate from abridged play and earlier chapter saves.
  • Provide shorter ways to explore the game without requiring the full campaign.
  • Let different encounter modes share characters and equipment effects.

Scope & results

The browser experience is the project’s actual original opening demo. The newer ten-chapter campaign is a separate, larger build. Both are projects for testing and iteration, rather than commercially finished releases.

What I built

The ceremonial mace was designed for Greenville Technical College by Abdurrahman Housari, Andrew Duvall, and Adam McArdle. The CAD view and photograph show the relationship between the digital design, its metal components, and the finished piece on a wooden stand.

My contribution

Contributed more than 100 hours of CAD and design-for-manufacturability iteration with machinists, supporting fabrication and finishing of the multi-part brass/aluminum assembly.

  • A complete multi-part CAD design.
  • Collaboration with machinists on manufacturability.
  • Brass and aluminum components with contrasting finishes.
  • A finished ceremonial object and presentation stand.

Components

Design

Multi-part CAD model, proportions, interfaces, and presentation.

Manufacturing collaboration

Design-for-manufacture iteration, fabrication support, and finishing.

Design choices

  • Iterate the design with the people who would manufacture the parts.
  • Consider component interfaces, proportions, and material finishes together.
  • Carry the design through fabrication and final presentation.

Scope & results

Designed with Andrew Duvall and Adam McArdle, with machinists collaborating on fabrication and finishing.

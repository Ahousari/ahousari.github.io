What I built

This collection brings together applied and exploratory vision work. Cookie Vision measures cookies from calibrated photos; Cookie Studio combines photo measurements, design preparation, print previews, and a printer-control interface that starts in simulation. Related ECE 4310 projects explore how images become regions, tracks, poses, and geometric measurements.

My contribution

Developed application interfaces and experiments across image processing, pretrained inference, tracking, and camera geometry. The collection keeps individual implementations distinct while explaining the methods and engineering questions they share.

  • Cookie-photo perspective calibration, cookie measurements, design preparation, and printer simulation.
  • Detection and transfer-learning exercises using pretrained models.
  • Segmentation, active contours, pose, and multi-object tracking.
  • Camera calibration, camera-pose estimation, and stereo depth exploration.

Components

Cookie Vision + Cookie Studio

Camera/photo preparation, cookie design, and printer-control interfaces.

Detection & segmentation

YOLO, transfer learning, SAM/instance masks, color regions, and active contours.

Tracking & pose

HSV tracking, Kalman filtering, optimal assignment, and body/hand/face pose.

Calibration & stereo

Camera intrinsics, pose measurement, rectification, correspondence, and estimated depth.

Design choices

  • Show intermediate evidence such as masks, matches, and coordinate systems.
  • Distinguish a model prediction from measured ground truth.
  • Evaluate association and calibration failures as part of the result.

Scope & results

The browser example uses a generated test image and simple color thresholding. Cookie Vision currently uses photos; live-camera work is planned. Cookie Studio starts in simulation, and prepared firmware still needs physical hardware/calibration tests. The collection’s implementations are separate coursework and application projects.

What I built

Folio prepares English, Arabic, and bilingual PDFs for reading and listening. It combines text extraction and OCR with generated narration, passage navigation, bookmarks, and saved reading progress. Prepared editions can be exported or downloaded for offline use.

My contribution

Developed the reading and preparation workflows, language and narration controls, resumable preparation, edition exports, and device-side reading experience. The pipeline retains completed passages and exposes a narration sample before longer processing.

  • Selectable-text extraction with OCR when needed.
  • English/Arabic reading views and optional machine translation.
  • Generated narration with bookmarks, resume, and a sleep timer.
  • HTML, text, continuous audio, read-along video, and portable book archives.

Components

Preparation

PDF extraction, OCR, narration, optional translation, and resumable processing.

Reading & portability

Passage views, progress, bookmarks, offline editions, and exports.

Design choices

  • Let users check a short narration sample before committing to a full book.
  • Preserve completed work so interrupted preparation can resume.
  • Keep the original text and narration when adding another reading edition.

Scope & results

This browser reading edition supports sample or pasted text, passage navigation, bookmarks, saved progress, and browser read-aloud. PDF extraction, OCR, and the original local narration pipeline belong to the full application. Browser voices are separate from Folio’s prepared narration models.

What I built

My Rotation is a personal music application with a computer-hosted master library and a mobile web interface. It combines organizing and listening with importing, trimming, processing, and keeping selected recordings available offline.

My contribution

Developed the library and playlist workflows, playback preferences, account-scoped device storage, deferred edits and additions, background audio jobs, and distribution/update tooling. The computer handles audio processing while the phone can retain finished tracks for offline listening.

  • Playlist membership without duplicating the underlying audio.
  • Weighted shuffle with recent-play adjustments.
  • Offline downloads and queued changes that synchronize after reconnecting.
  • Trimming, encoding, silence cleanup, vocal separation, and previous audio versions.

Components

Listening

Playlists, preference weights, artwork, playback, and downloads.

Processing & sync

Background audio work, original-source edits, offline changes, and reconnect handling.

Design choices

  • Keep original recordings so later processing can begin from the source.
  • Give retried additions stable identifiers to prevent duplicate jobs.
  • Separate an offline-ready interface from the computer’s availability.

Scope & results

This browser edition supports sample playback, visitor-imported audio, local playlists, preference weights, and WAV trimming. Imported recordings stay on the visitor’s device. The full application additionally provides computer-hosted importing, advanced processing, accounts, and cross-device sync.

What I built

This prototype explores how a single-screen HMI can communicate a DC microgrid’s equipment states and power flows. Three illustrative scenarios give the interface a concrete way to explain normal operation, a grid outage, and an emergency disconnect.

My contribution

Developed the proposal display and scenario-based interface. The prototype makes the chosen operating state visible through equipment labels, flows, and illustrative readings.

  • Normal-operation, outage, and disconnect views.
  • A single-screen arrangement of equipment and status.
  • Illustrative power and battery information.

Components

Proposal display

Equipment layout, state labels, illustrative readings, and scenario selection.

Design choices

  • Keep operating-state labels visible beside the diagram.
  • Use a limited set of scenarios to explain the concept.
  • Treat this DC proposal separately from IEEE 68-bus AC recordings.

Scope & results

All values, device states, controls, and thresholds are simulated. No equipment is connected. This is an interface concept, not a validated engineering model, operational SCADA installation, or safety system.

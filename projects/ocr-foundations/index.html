What I built

This collection records the early building blocks behind later vision work. Lab 1 processes a handwritten alphabet into normalized letter images and compares template matching with structural feature constraints. Selected earlier lecture programs explore slope prediction, MNIST, and CIFAR-10.

My contribution

Developed the thresholding, cropping, labeling, template comparison, feature counting, annotation, and paragraph-recognition tools. Compared baseline and modified methods with saved predictions and confusion matrices.

  • 286 handwritten letter images, with 260 training and 26 isolated-letter test samples.
  • Normalized cross-correlation and endpoint/branchpoint constraints.
  • Paragraph annotation and fixed/multiscale local matching.
  • Introductory regression and image-classification programs.

Components

Lab 1 toolchain

Threshold Tool, Letter Labeler, Feature Classifier, NCC Classifier, Paragraph Annotator, Paragraph OCR, and Multiscale OCR.

Earlier lectures

Selected Lectures 2–11, including slope prediction, MNIST, and CIFAR-10.

Design choices

  • Normalize letter crops while preserving their aspect ratio.
  • Measure whether added structural constraints actually help.
  • Examine scale and alignment when isolated-letter methods fail on paragraphs.

Scope & results

The isolated-letter baseline scored 16/26 (61.54%); structural constraints scored 10/26. Multiscale local paragraph matching improved 4/42 to 10/42. The small generated glyph demo below illustrates template comparison, not the original evaluation.
